I'm Mehluli Nokwara — AI engineer, founder, and MS Artificial Intelligence student at Yeshiva University. I've spent five years building production systems across healthcare, e-commerce, and AI platforms. I founded two companies: Menokws, which built IoT medical devices deployed across 20+ clinics in Zimbabwe and won the POTRAZ National Hackathon with $50K in funding, and TrendPulse, a live Shopify AI forecasting app for fashion and beauty brands. My background spans the full stack — from FreeRTOS firmware on STM32 microcontrollers and custom PCB design, to LLM applications, RAG systems, and cloud infrastructure on AWS.

My Experience

I've worked across healthcare software, e-commerce platforms, and AI systems. Each role has deepened my ability to build reliable, scalable backend systems and apply machine learning to real-world problems.

Founder & AI Engineer

TrendPulse2025 – Present

Architected and shipped a production Shopify embedded app for inventory demand forecasting serving fashion and beauty brands. Achieved RMSE of 1.9 on the Walmart M5 forecasting benchmark. Built end-to-end ML pipelines and real-time prediction APIs deployed as containerized services on AWS. Onboarded first pilot merchants and iterated the system based on real-world buying behavior.

Founder & Lead Engineer

Menokws Healthcare Systems2021 – 2023

Founded and led Menokws from idea to funded startup. Built low-cost IoT medical devices deployed across 20+ clinics. Engineered real-time firmware using FreeRTOS with C/C++ on STM32F4 microcontrollers. Developed proprietary PPG algorithms for heart rate and SpO2 monitoring. Designed custom PCBs and built a scalable RESTful API backend for secure IoT data relay. Won the POTRAZ National Hackathon, Healthcare Innovation category, awarded ZWG 5M (~$50K) in funding.

Software Systems Engineer
